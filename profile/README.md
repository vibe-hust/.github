VIBE Lab is a research group at [Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)](https://www.hust.edu.cn/). We develop generative intelligence systems that connect large-scale models with real-world perception and action.

## Research

- **World Models** — Predictive representations for long-horizon video generation, planning, and embodied interaction.
- **Generative AI** — Diffusion, flow matching, and efficient generative architectures.
- **Vision & Embodied AI** — Large vision models, multimodal learning, robot learning, and sim-to-real embodied intelligence.

## People

The group is led by [Prof. Tao Hu](https://taohu.me/), a Full Professor at HUST. Meet our researchers and collaborators on the [VIBE Lab website](https://taohu.me/lab/#group-members).
